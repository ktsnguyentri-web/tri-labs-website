## Overview
Adaptive Façade Solver is an algorithmic envelope optimization suite built on Rhino, Grasshopper, and Python. It automates the calculation of solar insolation across complex double-curved architectural skins and computes panel aperture angles to minimize heat gain while maximizing daylight autonomy.

## Computational Architecture
- **Insolation Mesh Analysis**: Real-time ray-tracing using Ladybug and custom NumPy matrix operations.
- **Kinetic Louver Mapping**: Parametric attractor curves controlling panel rotation and perforation density.
- **Panel Rationalization**: Planarization of quad panels to optimize manufacturing cost and structural fabrication.
