## The Paradigm Shift in Generative AI
For decades, CAD and computational architecture relied on explicit procedural modeling. Generative AI initially entered the field through 2D diffusion models that suffered from geometric inconsistency and lack of spatial awareness.

## World Models as Architectural Tools
World models understand physical constraints: persistence of mass, gravity, occlusion, and topological connectivity. For architects, this enables direct generation of coherent 3D volumetric representations rather than superficial rendering passes.

## Looking Forward
As generative world models integrate directly with parametric constraint engines (Grasshopper, Dynamo), the design process will evolve from manual drafting to intentional steering of generative spatial possibilities.
