## The Evolution of the Toolmaker
Architects have historically oscillated between craftspersons and system designers. The introduction of computational design in the late 1990s created the architect-programmer. In 2026, we are witnessing another profound transition: the rise of the architect-director.

## Agentic Workflows in Practice
Rather than scripting every individual node in a Grasshopper canvas, modern computational architects instruct specialized agents to execute complex parametric optimizations, evaluate structural load simulations, and generate production-ready documentation sets.

## Human Sensibility & Guardrails
Computational power without editorial discipline produces noise. The architect's primary contribution remains cultural resonance, spatial nuance, and setting the rigorous guardrails within which computational algorithms flourish.
